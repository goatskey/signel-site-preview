High-visibility medallion

Product ID 113563
Categories: Promotions & new products / New products

Pricing depends on quantity and specification. Call or email us with the product code and we will come back to you with a quote.

1 (833) 867-0960 info@signel.ca

Description

Make young people more visible on the move with this lightweight, practical and easy-to-attach reflective medallion. Its reflective surface catches headlight beams to help make the wearer more visible as the light fades.

Easy to attach to a school bag, a lunch box, a zipper or a key ring, it goes with them every day, above all on the way to school, on foot or by bike.

A simple, affordable solution, ideal for distribution at scale:

  • Schools and school service centres
  • Municipalities
  • Road safety campaigns
  • Student visibility programs
  • Youth activities and events

Customization available with your logo, your message or your visual identity.

(Minimum order of 50 units)
