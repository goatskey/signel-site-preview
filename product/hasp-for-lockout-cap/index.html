Description

Hasps for compression lockout plugs, taking three padlocks.

  • During water main work, several safety steps have to be carried out to prevent a section being put back into service by accident.
  • The safety cover lets the worker operate the valves from the surface, so there is no need to enter a confined space and no risk of falling in.
  • Once the valve is closed, the lockout device is placed in the opening of the valve chamber.
  • That precaution stops human error from putting workers' safety at risk.
