Description

Our FEU BOSS lets you protect your flaggers in line with Tome V of the Ministère des Transports du Québec, following CNESST recommendations, to suit your needs and at lower cost. Whatever your work vehicle, whatever the shape of your work zones, whatever your budget, this system gives you safe control of work zones large and small.

– Keeps traffic moving safely
– Adaptable, ergonomic and compact system
– High visibility that encourages drivers to slow down
– 3 mounting choices
– Activated nearby and/or remotely
– Protection mechanism if it meets an obstacle
– Low power draw
– Two slots for a flagger flag

Mechanical specifications:

  • Aluminum light head for 8in (200mm) red and yellow lamps
  • Barricade fitted with a flagger flag and retroreflective bands, compliant with Tome V
  • 400 lb actuator, so it stays reliable in winter and high winds.

Mounting options

  • Sliding mount for a vehicle hitch (RSS80)
  • Multi-purpose EFFIA trailer (RSS60)
  • Mobile battery housing, work zone light style (RSS44 | RSS46)
  • Fitted in the box of a truck

Activation options included:

  • Single barricade controlled by a wireless key fob – 2 included | FBSx – FBSxR
  • Single barricade controlled by a wired push button – 1 included | FBSx – FBSxR

Additional activation options:

  • Single barricade controlled by a wired controller – fitted at the factory | FBSx – FBSxR
  • Barricades in a system controlled by a long-range LoRa controller, allowing radio communication between flagger lights (one to four barricades), compatible with FBSxR

Dimensions

  • Height: 72'' (1829mm)
  • Length with the full-length barricade: 103'' (2600mm)
  • Length with the half-length barricade: 69'' (1700mm)
  • Weight: 38 lb (17.3 kg)
