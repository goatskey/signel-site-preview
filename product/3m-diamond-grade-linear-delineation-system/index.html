Description

3M™ linear delineation panels are designed to give drivers a clear visual guide through work zones and in difficult road conditions. Made from 3M™ Diamond Grade™ reflective sheeting laminated onto thin aluminum, these corrugated panels return light strongly across a wide range of entrance and observation angles. The panels come in bright and fluorescent colours for better visibility by day and by night. Use 3M™ linear delineation panels to draw attention to a modified or changing roadway; they can be mounted on surfaces such as concrete barriers and metal guardrails.
