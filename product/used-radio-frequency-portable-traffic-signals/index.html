Description

This product is used. Condition and appearance may differ slightly from the photo.

Kit with one FF10 light head and housing. Battery not included.

ELECTRICAL SPECIFICATIONS

  • Synchronization held by radio frequency (RF) or by crystal
  • Lights can be set up to 1500m apart
  • Light heads with 3 LED lamps
  • 3-character colour countdown, "888" type, brightness set by photocell
  • Handheld controller with a liquid crystal display
  • 2, 3 or 4 light configuration:
    • Shared lane, 2 lights
    • Intersection, 3 or 4 lights
    • Turning movement, 3 or 4 lights
  • Polycarbonate light head with an 8” (203mm) wide-dispersion lens
  • Polycarbonate countdown housing
  • Folding mast on a locked battery housing, moved on 2 wheels
  • Painted steel frame, orange
  • ABS controller case with a removable protective boot

PROGRAMMING

  • Controlled from the key controller
    • Sync mode, programmed light by light
    • Radio mode, lights programmed remotely
    • Sequential mode, sequence switched by the operator on site
  • 5 or 7 day schedule for allocating cycle time across a time window
  • Cycle time adjustable for each light in the circuit
  • Cycle time set from the distance between lights and the traffic volume
  • Controller shows:
    • Battery voltage readings
    • All-red mode on
    • Standby
  • Flashing red display under error conditions

Dimensions:

  • Mast height deployed: 107'' (2718 mm) Mast height folded: 63 1/2'' (161 mm)
  • Width: 20 1/8” (511 mm)
  • Length: 44 11/16'' (1135 mm)
  • Approximate weight: 120 Lbs (54.4 kg) without batteries

Options:

  • Controller CGEFF10
  • Battery charger: OP-A05
  • Battery: PS2001
  • Pneumatic damper: OP-A04
  • Vehicle detection by loop or radar for a green request
  • More than 4 lights
  • 120V input
  • Antennas for distances beyond 1500 m or noisy environments
  • Solar panels: OP-A33
