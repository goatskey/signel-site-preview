Description

These driver feedback signs are the best performing speed displays in their class and bring speeds down measurably from the first days after they go in.
They also collect data such as the number of vehicles and the average speed over a period.

With the RAD60L, manage the display and download data wirelessly from a laptop, smartphone or tablet by connecting to your radar's Wi-Fi while nearby.

Our RAD60 allows the display to be managed and data downloaded remotely over the cellular modem included. A subscription to our RADCLOUD platform gives you access to your radar signs wherever you are.

Electrical specifications

  • 7-segment speed display, 1-8-8 in three colours (green-yellow-red).
  • Full-matrix driver feedback display, 16 x 48 pixels, amber.
  • Display brightness adjusts to the ambient light.
  • Display switched off above a threshold, or for silent data collection.
  • Radar range up to 250m. Measures oncoming or two-way traffic.
  • Choice of power:
    • 120VAC mains: AC-DC converter included, no battery.
    • 347VAC lighting: ballast and battery charger for one 12V-10Ah AGM battery.
    • Solar: 100W panel for 2 AGM batteries of 12V-24Ah each. 5 days of autonomy with no solar input.

Mechanical specifications

  • UV-resistant ABS housing, IP65
  • 1/8'' (3mm) polycarbonate window.
  • Aluminum bracket and stainless steel mounting hardware.
  • Operating temperature -40⁰C to 65⁰C.
